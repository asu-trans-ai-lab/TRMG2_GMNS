For your MPObring your network, keep your coefficients

You provideWhat goes in

  • A GMNS network — node.csv + link.csv (centroids first, sorted by from-node)
  • Zonal socioeconomic data as CSV — households, population, employment
  • Your choice coefficients — or reuse a calibrated set; nothing is invented for you
  • Optionally, a transit level-of-service matrix to unlock the transit terms

You get backWhat comes out

  • A full four-step forecast + four-period equilibrium assignment
  • The flow-through operators (Δ, A, Π) — reload to reproduce instantly
  • A gradient path from link counts back to demand parameters
  • Open fidelity gates comparing every purpose to your survey targets
