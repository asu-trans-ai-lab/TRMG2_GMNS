A common situation: you have an OD demand from the first three steps, but not the model owner's official pre-assignment matrix. How do you know your OD is reasonable? You assign it and compare the loaded network to observed traffic counts — no extra input needed. This page teaches the method and its order; the numbers are reviewed with the model owners.

The order is the lessonmagnitude first · pattern second
STEP ① · FIRST, ALWAYS

Magnitude bias

bias = ( Σ modeled − Σ counted ) / Σ counted

Is the total loaded volume in the right ballpark? This is the first and decisive mark.

If the bias is large, the OD is the wrong size — missing trips or markets — and you stop here and refine. No correlation rescues a model that is only a fraction of observed volume.
→
STEP ② · ONLY AFTER ①

Pattern

scale to equal totals, then compare shape

Once magnitude is in range, does the OD load the network in the right proportions? Read scale-adjusted %RMSE, correlation R², GEH — by facility type, area type, and screenline.

Pattern localizes where any remaining shortfall sits — it is a diagnosis, not a pass/fail.
▲ do not read Step ② while a large Step ① bias stands ▲
The pattern metricsstep ② toolbox
scale-adjusted %RMSE — shape error with the total normalized out; comparable across models of different size.
correlation R² — do high-count links carry high modeled volume?
GEH < 5 (%) — the traffic-engineering closeness measure; share of links that pass.
by facility / area / screenline — localizes the shortfall (freeways carry through & commercial travel a resident-only OD omits).
The target to aim forTRMG2's own published validation

The bar to hit is the model owner's reported accuracy. Freeways validate tightest (few, high-volume, well-counted); local streets loosest (many, low-volume, noisy) — a universal shape. A reproduction is doing well when its facility-type profile approaches this, after the magnitude bias is closed.

When the official OD arrives. A direct OD-to-OD check — matrix %RMSE, coincidence ratio, 12-district cell agreement — is added on top via validate_vs_matrix(). The count-based validation above never depended on it, and stays the first line of verification. Method & code: od_validation.py, OD_VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK.md.
