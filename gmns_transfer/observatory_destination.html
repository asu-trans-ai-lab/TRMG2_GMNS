Given a trip, where does it go? TRMG2 uses a nested logit: a traveler first picks a district, then a zone within it — which captures how people know some parts of a region better than others. The Triangle is split into 12 districts. ✓ reproduced this two-level nested choice is reproduced from the source; the matrix below is our own AM result.

61% of AM trips stay inside their own district (the bright diagonal) — people travel locally. Of the rest, Raleigh is the dominant magnet: the Northeast, Southeast, Garner and Cary all pour into it. That radial pull is the region's structure, read straight off the model.
